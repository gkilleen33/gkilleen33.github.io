Economics Search Engine

A Google custom search engine that searches articles from all AEA journals, University of Chicago Press journals, the QJE, REStud, Econometrica, and the JDE. After searching, you can refine results to only the AER, QJE, REStud, JPE, Econometrica, or JDE. Suggestions are welcome by email.
